# Business-Logic Document - Orderly

Source: sample-repo @ 2026-09-10; scope: all; stack: TypeScript / Express 4 / Objection+Knex / PostgreSQL 16. Generated by explore-business-logic.

## Domain overview
Orderly is the order and stock management API of a small Dutch B2B wholesaler. Trade customers place orders for catalog products; staff confirm orders (which reserves stock and checks the customer's credit limit), take card payment, ship via a carrier and mark delivery. A nightly job reminds customers of unpaid confirmed orders. All money is in euros; VAT is charged for Dutch and Belgian customers and zero-rated for everyone else.

## Workflows
### Create customer
- **Trigger:** `POST /customers` (`src/routes/catalog.routes.ts:9`)
- **Actors:** staff (shared API key)
- **Steps:** 1. validate input (BR-010, BR-011); 2. reject duplicate email (BR-001); 3. insert customer
- **Entities touched:** read: Customer; written: Customer
- **Terminal outcomes:** created; 400 validation error; error "email already exists"

### Place and confirm order
- **Trigger:** `POST /orders` then `POST /orders/:id/confirm` (`src/routes/orders.routes.ts:7-8`)
- **Actors:** staff
- **Steps:** 1. create draft with volume-discounted line totals (BR-002); 2. on confirm, compute VAT by customer country (BR-003); 3. credit check (BR-004); 4. reserve stock per line (BR-005); 5. set status confirmed and placed_at
- **Entities touched:** read: Customer, Product; written: Order, OrderLine, Product
- **Terminal outcomes:** confirmed; error credit limit exceeded; error insufficient stock; error product unavailable

### Take payment
- **Trigger:** `POST /orders/:id/pay` (`src/routes/orders.routes.ts:9`)
- **Actors:** staff
- **Steps:** 1. require status confirmed (BR-006); 2. charge card in EUR via Stripe; 3. record Payment; 4. on success set paid_at and status paid
- **Entities touched:** read: Order; written: Payment, Order
- **Terminal outcomes:** paid; 402 payment failed (Payment row with status failed)

### Ship and deliver
- **Trigger:** `POST /orders/:id/ship`, `POST /orders/:id/deliver` (`src/routes/orders.routes.ts:11-12`)
- **Actors:** warehouse staff
- **Steps:** 1. require status paid (BR-007); 2. insert Shipment; 3. transition to shipped and e-mail customer (BR-008); 4. on deliver, stamp delivered_at and transition to delivered
- **Entities touched:** read: Order, Customer; written: Shipment, Order
- **Terminal outcomes:** shipped; delivered; 409 wrong status

### Cancel order
- **Trigger:** `POST /orders/:id/cancel`
- **Actors:** staff
- **Steps:** 1. allowed only from draft or confirmed (BR-006); 2. if confirmed, release reserved stock (BR-005); 3. set cancelled
- **Entities touched:** read: Order, OrderLine; written: Order, Product
- **Terminal outcomes:** cancelled; error invalid transition

### Payment reminder job
- **Trigger:** cron `0 9 * * *` server time (`src/jobs/reminder.job.ts:10`)
- **Actors:** scheduler
- **Steps:** 1. select confirmed orders unpaid for more than 7 days (BR-009); 2. e-mail each customer in English with the EUR total
- **Entities touched:** read: Order, Customer; written: none
- **Terminal outcomes:** e-mails sent; failures are logged only

## Business rules
- **BR-001** - Customer e-mail addresses are unique (case-insensitive).
  - Entities: Customer
  - Enforced at: `src/services/customer.service.ts:7` (application check only)
  - Notes: no unique index in the migration.
- **BR-002** - Lines of 100+ units get 5% off, 500+ get 10% off; line totals are rounded to cents.
  - Entities: OrderLine, Product
  - Enforced at: `src/services/pricing.service.ts:5-8`
- **BR-003** - VAT is 21% for NL and BE customers, 19% for DE, 0% otherwise, applied to the subtotal at confirmation.
  - Entities: Order, Customer
  - Enforced at: `src/controllers/orders.controller.ts:20-24`
  - Notes: a second, different rate table exists in `src/integrations/tax.provider.ts:3-6` (no DE) and is never called.
- **BR-004** - An order can only be confirmed if the customer's open unpaid exposure plus the order total is within the credit limit.
  - Entities: Order, Customer
  - Enforced at: `src/services/customer.service.ts:27`
- **BR-005** - Confirming reserves stock per line and fails if stock is insufficient or the product inactive; cancelling a confirmed order releases it.
  - Entities: Order, OrderLine, Product
  - Enforced at: `src/services/inventory.service.ts:7-9`, `src/services/order.service.ts:44-49`
- **BR-006** - Order status follows draft -> confirmed -> paid -> shipped -> delivered, with cancellation from draft or confirmed only.
  - Entities: Order
  - Enforced at: `src/services/order.service.ts:8-15,40`
  - Notes: `orders.controller.ts:33` and `shipments.controller.ts:12` re-check the pre-state before calling the transition.
- **BR-007** - Only paid orders can be shipped.
  - Entities: Order, Shipment
  - Enforced at: `src/controllers/shipments.controller.ts:12`
- **BR-008** - The customer is e-mailed when the order ships.
  - Entities: Order, Customer
  - Enforced at: `src/services/order.service.ts:55-57`
- **BR-009** - Confirmed orders unpaid after 7 days trigger a reminder e-mail.
  - Entities: Order, Customer
  - Enforced at: `src/jobs/reminder.job.ts:11-18`
- **BR-010** - Order quantities are whole numbers between 1 and 10,000.
  - Entities: OrderLine
  - Enforced at: `src/validators/order.schema.ts:5`
- **BR-011** - Customer postcode must match the Dutch format (4 digits + 2 letters); phone must be 6-15 digits with optional +.
  - Entities: Customer
  - Enforced at: `src/validators/order.schema.ts:16-19`
- **BR-012** - All amounts are in EUR and card charges are made in EUR.
  - Entities: Order, Payment
  - Enforced at: `src/integrations/payment.gateway.ts:9`
  - Notes: implied everywhere else; no currency column exists.

## Assumptions and gaps
1. No users, roles or tenants exist; one shared API key gates everything. Multi-tenancy is assumed absent.
2. Time zone: timestamps are naive `timestamp` columns and the container sets TZ=Europe/Amsterdam; the reminder cron runs in server time.
3. The tax provider module appears to be dead code; which rate table is authoritative could not be determined.
4. No invoice document is generated anywhere; "order" is the only commercial document.
5. No returns, refunds or credit notes exist in code.
6. All user-facing strings are English literals in code.
