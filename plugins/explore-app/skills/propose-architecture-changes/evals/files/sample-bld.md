# Business-Logic Document - Orderly

Source: sample-repo @ 2026-09-10; scope: all; stack: TypeScript / Express 4 / Objection+Knex / PostgreSQL 16. Generated by explore-business-logic, then edited to add the rules required for the Germany, UAE and Pakistan launch (BR-013 onward).

## Domain overview
Orderly is the order and stock management API of a small Dutch B2B wholesaler. Trade customers place orders on credit; staff confirm orders (credit check and stock reservation), take payment, ship via a carrier and mark delivery. A nightly job reminds customers of unpaid orders. The product is being launched in Germany, the UAE and Pakistan, each with its own tax regime, currency, payment methods and e-invoicing obligations.

## Workflows
### Place and confirm order
- **Trigger:** `POST /orders`, then `POST /orders/:id/confirm` (`src/routes/orders.routes.ts:7-8`)
- **Actors:** staff
- **Steps:** 1. create draft with discounted line totals (BR-002, `order.service.ts:21`); 2. compute VAT by customer country in the controller (BR-003, `orders.controller.ts:20-24`); 3. credit check (BR-004); 4. reserve stock (BR-005); 5. set confirmed
- **Entities touched:** read: Customer, Product; written: Order, OrderLine, Product
- **Terminal outcomes:** confirmed; credit limit exceeded; insufficient stock

### Take payment
- **Trigger:** `POST /orders/:id/pay` (`orders.controller.ts:31`)
- **Actors:** staff
- **Steps:** 1. require confirmed (BR-006); 2. charge via Stripe in EUR (BR-012); 3. insert Payment; 4. set paid_at and transition to paid
- **Entities touched:** read: Order; written: Payment, Order
- **Terminal outcomes:** paid; 402 payment failed

### Fulfil order (ship and deliver)
- **Trigger:** `POST /orders/:id/ship` and `POST /orders/:id/deliver` (`shipments.controller.ts:9,25`)
- **Actors:** warehouse staff
- **Steps:** 1. controller checks status paid (BR-007); 2. controller inserts Shipment; 3. controller calls OrderService.transition('shipped'), which e-mails the customer inline (BR-008); 4. on deliver, controller stamps delivered_at then transitions to delivered
- **Entities touched:** read: Order, Customer; written: Shipment, Order
- **Terminal outcomes:** shipped; delivered; 409 wrong status. If the transition fails after the shipment row is inserted, the shipment exists for an unshipped order.

### Cancel order
- **Trigger:** `POST /orders/:id/cancel`
- **Actors:** staff
- **Steps:** 1. transition guard (BR-006); 2. release stock if confirmed (BR-005); 3. set cancelled
- **Entities touched:** read: Order, OrderLine; written: Order, Product
- **Terminal outcomes:** cancelled; invalid transition

### Payment reminder job
- **Trigger:** cron `0 9 * * *` server time (`src/jobs/reminder.job.ts:10`)
- **Actors:** scheduler
- **Steps:** 1. select confirmed orders unpaid > 7 days (BR-009, own query, not via OrderService); 2. e-mail in English with EUR total
- **Entities touched:** read: Order, Customer; written: none
- **Terminal outcomes:** e-mails sent

## Business rules
- **BR-001** - Customer e-mail addresses are unique.
  - Entities: Customer
  - Enforced at: `src/services/customer.service.ts:7` (application check only)
- **BR-002** - Volume discounts: 5% at 100+, 10% at 500+.
  - Entities: OrderLine
  - Enforced at: `src/services/pricing.service.ts:5-8`
- **BR-003** - VAT is 21% for NL and BE, 19% for DE, 0% otherwise, applied at confirmation.
  - Entities: Order, Customer
  - Enforced at: `src/controllers/orders.controller.ts:20-24`
  - Notes: a second rate table in `src/integrations/tax.provider.ts:3-6` disagrees (no DE) and is never called.
- **BR-004** - Confirmation requires open exposure plus order total within credit limit.
  - Entities: Order, Customer
  - Enforced at: `src/services/customer.service.ts:27`
  - Notes: "open" is defined here and again, differently, in `reminder.job.ts:12` (status confirmed only).
- **BR-005** - Confirmation reserves stock; cancellation of a confirmed order releases it.
  - Entities: OrderLine, Product
  - Enforced at: `src/services/inventory.service.ts:7-9`, `src/services/order.service.ts:44-49`
- **BR-006** - Status transitions follow the lifecycle; invalid transitions are rejected.
  - Entities: Order
  - Enforced at: `src/services/order.service.ts:8-15,40`
  - Notes: controllers also pre-check status and patch `paid_at` directly (`orders.controller.ts:33,45`).
- **BR-007** - Only paid orders can be shipped.
  - Entities: Order, Shipment
  - Enforced at: `src/controllers/shipments.controller.ts:12`
- **BR-008** - The customer is e-mailed when the order ships.
  - Entities: Order, Customer
  - Enforced at: `src/services/order.service.ts:55-57` (synchronous, inside the transition)
- **BR-009** - Confirmed orders unpaid after 7 days get a reminder.
  - Entities: Order, Customer
  - Enforced at: `src/jobs/reminder.job.ts:11-18`
- **BR-010** - Quantities are whole numbers 1..10,000.
  - Entities: OrderLine
  - Enforced at: `src/validators/order.schema.ts:5`
- **BR-011** - Postcode must be Dutch format; phone a single digits regex.
  - Entities: Customer
  - Enforced at: `src/validators/order.schema.ts:16-19`
- **BR-012** - All amounts are EUR; card charges are in EUR.
  - Entities: Order, Payment
  - Enforced at: `src/integrations/payment.gateway.ts:9`
- **BR-013** - Tax is computed per customer market from a maintained rate table (DE 19% VAT, UAE 5% VAT, Pakistan sales tax by province), and the rate used is stored on the order.
  - Entities: Order, Customer
  - Enforced at: not enforced
- **BR-014** - Confirmed orders for UAE and Pakistan customers must be submitted to the market's e-invoicing authority (FTA / FBR) and the submission reference stored; failure must not lose the submission.
  - Entities: Order
  - Enforced at: not enforced
- **BR-015** - Payments are taken with a market-appropriate provider (cards via Stripe in DE, mada/STC Pay in UAE, JazzCash/EasyPaisa in Pakistan) in the order's currency.
  - Entities: Payment
  - Enforced at: not enforced
- **BR-016** - Reminder e-mails are sent at 09:00 in the customer's time zone and in the customer's language.
  - Entities: Order, Customer
  - Enforced at: not enforced
- **BR-017** - Each customer belongs to a legal entity (market); data for UAE customers must be stored in a UAE-region database.
  - Entities: Customer, Order
  - Enforced at: not enforced

## Assumptions and gaps
1. No users, roles, tenants or legal entities exist in code; one shared API key.
2. Timestamps are naive; container TZ is Europe/Amsterdam.
3. No invoice document exists; e-invoicing is entirely new.
4. Which VAT table is authoritative (controller vs tax.provider) could not be determined.
