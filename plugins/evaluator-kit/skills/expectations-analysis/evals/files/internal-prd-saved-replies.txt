PRD: Saved Replies for the Support Inbox
Owner: Priya (PM, Support Tools)   Eng lead: TBD   Design: Marco
Status: Draft v2 — ready for eng review
Target: Q1

Background

Our support team answers ~1,800 tickets a week in the inbox we built last year. Agents told us in the last two retros that they're re-typing the same five or six answers dozens of times a day ("where's my order", "how do I reset my password", "can I change my shipping address"). Two agents keep a personal Google Doc of paste-ready answers. Average first-response time has crept from 2h10 to 3h40 over the last two quarters while ticket volume grew 30%. Leadership has asked us to get FRT back under 2h30 by end of Q1 without adding headcount.

Problem

Agents have no shared, fast way to insert a standard answer into a reply.

Goals

- Agents can insert a saved reply into the composer in under 3 seconds
- Saved replies can be shared across the team, not just personal
- Managers can see which replies are used most

Non-goals

- AI-generated replies (separate initiative, Q2)
- Changes to the ticket routing logic
- Rich media in replies (images, attachments) — text and links only for v1

Proposed solution

Add a "Saved replies" panel to the composer. Agents open it with a keyboard shortcut or a button, search by title, click to insert. Replies support simple placeholders: {{customer_first_name}}, {{order_number}}. Anyone can create a personal reply; managers can promote a personal reply to a team reply. Usage count is recorded per insert.

Open questions

- Should inserting a reply replace the composer contents or append? (Marco leans append.)
- Do we need folders/tags for replies, or is search enough for v1?
- What happens to a team reply when the agent who created it leaves?
- Placeholders: what if order_number is empty for a ticket — leave the placeholder, blank it, or block insert?

Requirements

R1. Agent can create, edit, delete a personal saved reply (title + body).
R2. Agent can search saved replies by title from the composer and insert with one click.
R3. Manager can promote a personal reply to team scope; team replies visible to all agents.
R4. Body supports {{customer_first_name}} and {{order_number}} placeholders, filled from the ticket.
R5. Each insert increments a usage counter visible to managers in a simple list.
R6. Keyboard shortcut (Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+R) opens the panel.
