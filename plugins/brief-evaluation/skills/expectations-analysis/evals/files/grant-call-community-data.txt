Open Call: Community Data Fellowship 2027
Hosted by the Lanterne Foundation · Awards of up to $40,000 · 8–10 fellows

About the fellowship

The Community Data Fellowship supports practitioners who help communities use their own data to make decisions. Past fellows have built a flood-risk map with a riverside neighbourhood association, trained library staff to run their own survey programme, and worked with a tenants' union to document repair response times. What these projects share is not a technology. It is that the community owned the question, the data, and the result.

We fund people, not products. We have seen too many proposals that describe a platform the applicant wants to build, with a community attached as a user base. Those are not a fit here. If your project would still make sense with a different community swapped in, it is probably a product.

What we are looking for

1. A specific community you already have a relationship with. Tell us how long you have worked with them and in what capacity. Letters from the community are welcome but not required; what we need is evidence that they asked for this, or shaped it with you.
2. A question the community wants answered, in their words if possible.
3. A plan for how the community will hold the data and the results after the fellowship ends. "We will publish a report" is not a plan. Who keeps the spreadsheet? Who can run the survey again next year without you?
4. What you will learn. Fellows are selected partly on how the fellowship will change their practice, not only on the project outcome.
5. A budget. Up to $40,000 over 12 months. We expect the majority to go to your time and to community participants' time. Equipment and software are fine where needed; we rarely fund more than 15% on tools.

What we are not looking for

- Projects where the applicant is the primary beneficiary of the data
- Research on a community rather than with one
- Platforms, apps, or dashboards as the deliverable (they can be a means, not the end)
- Projects that require the community to adopt new software to participate

How we review

Every application is read in full by two reviewers, at least one of whom has been a fellow. We score on: depth of the existing relationship (30%), clarity of the community's question (20%), the handover plan (30%), and what the fellow will learn (20%). Budget is checked for fit but not scored. Shortlisted applicants will have a 30-minute conversation with two reviewers; where possible, we ask that a community member join.

Application

- Narrative: up to 1,500 words, covering points 1–5 above in any order you like. We read the whole thing; headings help us.
- Budget: one page.
- One work sample: something you have made with a community before. A map, a report, a survey instrument, a workshop plan. Tell us in two sentences what your role was and what the community's role was.
- Optional: up to two letters from community members.

Deadline: 15 January. We confirm receipt within two days and notify everyone by 1 March.

Questions about fit are welcome at fellowship@lanterne.org before 20 December. We answer every one, and we publish anonymised answers on this page weekly.
